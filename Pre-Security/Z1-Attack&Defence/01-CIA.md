### CIA Triad — Short Notes

The **CIA Triad** is the foundation of cybersecurity. It focuses on protecting data and systems in three ways:

## 1. Confidentiality

 Ensures data is accessible only to **authorized users**.
  Protects private and sensitive information.
 Examples: Passwords, encryption, access controls.

# Bank Example

Only **you and authorized bank employees** should be able to see your bank account details and transaction history.

If an attacker gets access to your bank password and sees your account information, **Confidentiality is broken**.

**Memory:** Confidentiality = **Keep data Secret** 

## 2. Integrity

 Ensures data is **accurate and not changed without permission**.
 Protects data from unauthorized modification.

# Bank Example

You transfer **₹10,000** to your friend.

If an attacker changes the transaction and sends the money to their own account, the **data was modified without permission**.

Therefore, **Integrity is broken**.

**Memory:** Integrity = **Keep data Correct**


## 3. Availability

 Ensures data and services are **available when needed**.
 Protects against downtime and service disruption.

# Bank Example

You need to withdraw money, but the bank's online banking system is down because of a cyber attack.

Your money is safe and has not been changed, but you **cannot access it**.

Therefore, **Availability is broken**.

**Memory:** Availability = **Keep data Accessible**
